Clinical review: condition names and ICD-10 codes

When someone searches a drug in Rx Label Lookup, the “Used for” list is built from the table below. Each row turns wording found on an FDA label into an everyday name, a medical term and an ICD-10-CM code.

  1. Enter your name and role.
  2. For each row, check the everyday name, the medical term and the ICD-10-CM code. Choose Approve or Needs change, and write the correction in Notes.
  3. When you're done, choose Copy my feedback and paste it into an email or text to the person who sent you this link.

Your answers are saved in this browser only, so you can stop and come back later on the same device.
